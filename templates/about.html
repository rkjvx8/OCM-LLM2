About Dr. Jacobson

Recognized for change leadership, digital transformation, and enterprise adoption expertise.

Dr. Jacobson brings more than 15 years of experience helping executives and teams lead successful change, accelerate digital transformation, and strengthen workforce adoption in complex organizations.

Dr. Jacobson is a respected advisor in Change Management and Digital Transformation, known for combining rigorous organizational psychology with practical consulting, facilitation, and leadership enablement.

His work has supported clients such as Lockheed Martin, Disney, LinkedIn, Marathon Oil, Bayer, Leidos, Thermo Fisher, Ford, and public sector organizations where transformation success required strategic clarity, operational discipline, and measurable adoption outcomes.

He helps organizations move beyond implementation to create lasting behavior change, stronger leadership alignment, and sustainable performance improvement across the enterprise.
