About Ryan Jacobson

Bridging organizational psychology, change leadership, and Agile delivery.

Ryan brings more than 15 years of experience helping leaders and teams improve adoption, business readiness, and Agile performance during enterprise transformation.

Ryan’s practice blends academic grounding with practical consulting, facilitation, and training across industries where sustained change depends on people, leadership, and measurable outcomes.

His work has supported clients such as Lockheed Martin, Disney, LinkedIn, Marathon Oil, Bayer, Leidos, Thermo Fisher, Ford, and public sector organizations where transformation success required both strategic clarity and operational discipline.

He helps organizations move beyond project completion to ensure new ways of working are understood, adopted, and reinforced across teams.

  • Leadership development and team alignment
